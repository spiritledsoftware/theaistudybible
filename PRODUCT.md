# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is everyday Bible readers seeking accessible, consistent personal Scripture study. New or exploring readers are a secondary audience; the experience should help them engage without assuming extensive biblical knowledge.

## Product Purpose

The AI Study Bible helps readers understand a passage by keeping Scripture central and making grounded explanations and biblical connections available when needed. Success means users leave a study session able to understand and apply the passage more clearly.

## Positioning

Scripture-grounded AI study: AI assists understanding while Scripture—not generated commentary—remains the center of the experience.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, calm, illuminating. Interaction quality should take its cue from Apple Books: polished long-form reading, with controls that recede so the content stays in focus.

## Anti-references

Do not feel like a flashy AI demo. Avoid glows, gradients, constant motion, and AI theatrics that compete with Scripture.

## Design Principles

1. **Scripture remains primary.** AI context supports the passage and never becomes the main event.
2. **Protect reading flow.** Controls stay discoverable but recede when they are not needed.
3. **Offer depth progressively.** Everyday readers get an approachable default; deeper tools appear in context rather than crowding the page.
4. **Illuminate without performing.** Use calm clarity and meaningful feedback instead of visual AI spectacle.
5. **Make understanding tangible.** Every feature should help readers explain, connect, remember, or apply what they read.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Treat reading customization as a core requirement, including scalable type, comfortable line length and spacing, and usable theme controls.
